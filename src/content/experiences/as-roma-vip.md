A Roma home game at the Stadio Olimpico, done from the good side of the barrier. A chauffeur collects you at your hotel two hours before kickoff and drives you to the ground.

Inside, the hospitality area is open: a gourmet buffet with no limit on it, and a bar that stays open through the match. You get an official Roma scarf, and a host who is there for whatever you need all evening.

The seats are in the Tribuna d'Onore Sud — a section that is not sold at the ticket office or by any online vendor — so you watch the game with the Curva Sud in full voice below you. Afterwards the same chauffeur takes you back to the hotel.

Minimum age 12. Book at least 24 hours before kickoff.

Kickoff times move: you are told your exact pickup time as soon as the match time is confirmed, and the Serie A calendar can shift for policing reasons. Four VIP seats are held for every Serie A, Conference League and Coppa Italia game — for a larger party, ask and we will check.
