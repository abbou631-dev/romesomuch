Sit back in your own private electric golf cart and let a local driver-guide take you through the Rome you came to see and the Rome most visitors miss. No walking marathons, no buses, no queues to plan around: we pick you up at your hotel and shape the route around you.

In three hours you glide past the Colosseum, the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain, the Spanish Steps and Piazza Navona, then climb to the Aventine for the famous Keyhole view of St Peter's and the Orange Garden terrace, before winding back through the cobbled lanes of Trastevere. Along the way you stop for photos wherever you like and hear the stories behind the stones.

Want an espresso, a gelato or a glass of wine halfway? Just ask your guide. Perfect for couples, families with kids and anyone seeing Rome for the first time.

We pick you up at your hotel or any address in central Rome. You add the address when you book, and your driver will be waiting at the time you choose.

**Good to know**

- Departures every 30 minutes from 8:00 to 20:00
- 1 to 20 guests (larger groups travel in several carts)
- Suitable for all ages: little walking, ideal for families and guests who prefer not to walk far
