Hop into a classic Vespa sidecar and explore the city in true Italian style. Your local guide is also your driver, so you cruise past Rome's landmarks while the stories arrive with them.

Three hours of viewpoints, ancient monuments and quiet piazzas, aboard one of the most memorable ways to get around Rome.

Whether it's your first time in the city or your tenth, this is Rome from a different angle: stylish, intimate and entirely Italian.

The route runs Colosseum, Castel Sant'Angelo, St Peter's Square and Fontana dell'Acqua Paola, and ends at the Pantheon with gelato.
