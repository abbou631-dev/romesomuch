Get your hands dirty in a pasta-making class in the middle of Rome. A local chef shows you how to make fresh pasta from scratch with techniques handed down through generations. No experience needed, only an appetite.

While the dough rests there's a welcome aperitivo, then tiramisu, made by you as well. After that you sit down and eat what you made, which is the point of the whole afternoon.

The pasta contains gluten, eggs and dairy, so the class is not suitable for coeliacs or for anyone who cannot eat pasta. Tell us about allergies and intolerances when you book.
