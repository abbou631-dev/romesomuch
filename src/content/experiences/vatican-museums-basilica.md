Four hours to cover the three places everyone comes to Rome for, with priority entry to each and a guide who knows what to skip.

The Vatican Museums first: the Pio Clementino with the Laocoön and the Apollo Belvedere, then the Gallery of Maps, the Candelabra and the Tapestries. The Sistine Chapel next, for Michelangelo's Last Judgment. Then St Peter's Basilica, the largest church in Christendom, with Michelangelo's Pietà and Bernini's baldachin over the altar.

It finishes in St Peter's Square, under the cupola and inside Bernini's double colonnade.

Full names of every traveller are needed at booking. Dress code is enforced: knees and shoulders covered. Large backpacks, big umbrellas and sharp objects are not allowed in, and the route is not suitable for anyone with mobility difficulties or for strollers.
