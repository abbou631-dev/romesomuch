Make a one-of-a-kind pendant by learning the Roman micromosaic technique, worked in glass, in a professional mosaic studio in Rome.

A master mosaicist works beside you. You start with a short introduction to where micromosaics come from, then choose a silver pendant shape from a set designed by goldsmiths for this workshop.

From there it's texture, composition and cutting glass rods into tiny tiles, until the design you had in mind is set in silver. No experience needed, only curiosity.

Not suitable for children under 12.
