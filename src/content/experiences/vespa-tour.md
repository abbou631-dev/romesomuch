Ride through the streets of Rome like a true local on the back of a classic Vespa, driven by a friendly local guide. You'll see the city's most iconic sights and its hidden corners, picking up stories, views and the breeze of the Eternal City along the way.

Halfway through we stop at Two Sizes, one of the best tiramisu spots in Rome, for a break with the most Italian dessert there is.

The route runs Colosseum, Circus Maximus, Orange Garden, Gianicolo Hill and Fontana dell'Acqua Paola, with a drop-off at Piazza Navona. The Pantheon and Piazza Venezia can be added on request, traffic permitting.

Minimum age 8. Not suitable for pregnant women, wheelchair users or toddlers. You need no licence of any kind: the guide drives.
