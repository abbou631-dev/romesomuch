Only one day in Rome? This is the way to see all of it without wearing yourself out. Your private electric golf cart and local driver-guide pick you up at your hotel and take you across the whole city in six relaxed hours.

Start with the Colosseum and the Roman Forum, then weave through the centre to the Pantheon, the Trevi Fountain and the Spanish Steps. Cross the Tiber to St Peter's Square, climb the Janiculum Hill for the best panorama of Rome, and lose yourself in the lanes of Trastevere. Then end at the Orange Garden on the Aventine.

Halfway through we stop for lunch at a local favourite to taste authentic Roman stuffed pizza, which is included. Ideal for cruise passengers, short stays and families who want to see everything in one go.

We pick you up at your hotel or any address in central Rome.

**Good to know**

- Departures at 8:00, 9:00, 10:00, 13:00, 13:30, 14:00
- 1 to 20 guests
- Entrance tickets to monuments are not included (the tour is outdoors)
