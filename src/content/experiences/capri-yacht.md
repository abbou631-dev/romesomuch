Capri in a day, done properly: business-class seats on the fast train from Rome to Naples, a private chauffeur waiting to take you to the marina, and then a 2023 Tornado 38 that is yours alone until evening.

You cross the Bay of Naples with Vesuvius behind you and the island growing in front, then spend the day working around Capri's coast — the cliffs with their villas stacked above the water, the Faraglioni, the Blue Grotto, and the quieter Green and White ones that most boats skip. The skipper knows where to stop for a swim and where the crowds are not.

There is a sundeck with an awning for the hot hours, snorkels and towels on board, and drinks and snacks whenever you want them. Lunch you take at your leisure, ashore or on the water.

The day starts and ends at Roma Termini, in front of track 1.

Not suitable for wheelchairs, strollers or pets, and infants travel on your lap. If children are coming, tell us their ages when you book so the right life jackets are on board.
